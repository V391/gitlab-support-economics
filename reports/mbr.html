Headlines

  • October volume rose 13% on September and first reply SLA fell to 88.2%: the seasonal October peak pushed AMER and EMEA past the utilization ceiling. The same pattern hit January and March; FY28 staffing is sized to the peak month for this reason.
  • APAC SLA dipped below 95% from June to August: four engineers left in May and utilization went past the ~55% level where first reply SLA holds. Backfills started in September and SLA recovered.
  • FY28 plan: 100 engineers at today's AI footprint, 95 if self-service reaches every account, against 113 without AI. Sized to the peak month at the 55% utilization ceiling.
  • AI self-service is real but smaller than the bot says. Rollout accounts filed 20% fewer how-to and config tickets than the holdout (95% CI 15% to 24%): about 142 tickets a month. The assistant marks 613 conversations a month as resolved.
  • Agent assist saves 11% of handle time when used, not the 48% a used vs not used comparison shows: engineers reach for it on easy tickets.
  • secure & govern is the most expensive stage for product quality: $890k of support time in 12 months went to tickets that ended in an engineering escalation.
  • 81 renewing accounts ($34.79M ARR) flagged at risk by a support signal model (test AUC 0.80, top decile holds 40% of churn).
